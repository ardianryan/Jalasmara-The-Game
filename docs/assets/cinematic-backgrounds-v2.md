# Phase 1 cinematic backgrounds, v2

Five original environment plates were generated on 16 November 2026 for the Phase 1 cutscene pass. Ardian Ryan supplied the screenplay and visual direction; a Codex agent prompted and curated the project artwork with the built-in image generation tool. These are **candidate scenic backgrounds**, with no character poses baked in. The integrator must still connect the images to Godot scenes and review the moving composition. No moodboard image, third-party sprite pack, or existing game's frame was used as a distributable source.

Sources are `assets/source-2d/cinematics-v2/<scene>-source.png` (1672×941 each). Runtime assets are `client-godot/resources/art/cinematics-v2/<scene>.png` (960×540 PNG after metadata stripping). The export command for each plate was `magick SOURCE -filter point -resize 960x540\! -strip -define png:compression-level=9 RUNTIME`, using ImageMagick 7.1.2-31. Point sampling preserves the block edges for the game's nearest-neighbor presentation; the asset images do not define world collision or terrain geometry. The five runtime PNGs total 4,540,109 bytes before Godot import/compression.

## Prompt: Kedai Waras interior

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original 16:9 static background plate for the Godot pixel-art game Jala Smara, interior Kedai Waras cutscenes. Scene: wide three-quarter view inside a small Nusantara coastal stilt-house herbal tea tavern at night, empty of people. A sturdy timber serving counter and two low wooden tables create open staging space in the middle and lower half for separately animated character sprites. Handwoven bamboo wall panels, brass-rimmed clay cups, jars of herbs, a bamboo lantern, carved wood frames, a rain-streaked window showing teal water and distant violet mist. Style: polished, deliberately hand-clustered pixel-art diorama with clear large square pixel groups and sharp nearest-neighbor edges, authentic readable construction, restrained detail at small game display size. Palette: amber lantern warmth against cool teal rain and subtle plum shadows, tropical cozy and cheerful despite the storm; painterly light over crisp pixels, no grunge. Framing: wide establishing shot, wall and window in upper half, floor and counter in lower half, no close-up object blocking central stage. Constraints: environment only, no people, no creatures, no weapons, no writing, no signs, no logo, no UI, no watermark, no blurred pixel edges. Original visual design, not copied from any existing game.

## Prompt: Rongga Kapur Jalatunda

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: original 16:9 static cutscene background plate for a Godot underwater 2.5D pixel-art game, Rongga Kapur Jalatunda at 60–95 meters depth. Composition is a side-on underwater cave arena with layered parallax-like limestone walls receding behind a broad clear center-left swimming lane, rock shelf and softly glowing reef at lower edges. On the RIGHT cave wall, at about 80 percent of image width and 43 percent of image height, one heavy corroded industrial discharge pipe visibly ENTERS the solid limestone from outside and is physically bolted to it with steel brackets and rock shadows; its opening faces diagonally down-left. The pipe is clearly attached, supported, and ends at the wall contact rather than hanging in water. Only a little purple acid leakage appears near that opening, surrounded by natural turquoise bubbles. Further back a narrow ancient stone gate hints at a deeper area. Style: hand-clustered crisp pixel-art environment, readable large square pixels, coherent side-view depth, detailed but not speckled rocks and integrated coral, luminous teal and sea-green water with a subdued violet pollution accent, cool and mysterious yet beautiful and legible at 667x375. Sun rays fade from upper-left, no strong blur. Keep center-left empty for Nala and radio overlay. Constraints: no character, no creature, no labels, no logos, no text, no UI, no watermark, no floating or unsupported pipe, no photorealism. Original visual design.
