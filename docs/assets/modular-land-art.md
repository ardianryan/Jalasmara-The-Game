# Material dan vegetasi peta modular

Dua atlas orisinal dibuat dengan tool imagegen bawaan pada 8 November 2026. Berkas PNG keluaran disimpan tanpa perubahan pada `client-godot/resources/art/land-materials-v1.png` dan `land-foliage-v1.png`. Keduanya berukuran 1536 × 1024, dengan enam sel 512 × 512. Atlas material adalah RGB; vegetasi mempunyai alpha asli (lebih dari separuh piksel hampir transparan), bukan gambar latar hijau untuk dipakai sebagai lantai.

Atlas ini melengkapi material atau siluet yang belum tersedia pada aset pustaka. Penggunaan tile DCSS dan model Polyfork dicatat terpisah beserta lisensinya. Atlas tidak memuat gambar, susunan level, karakter, aksara atau simbol sakral dari game referensi.

Generate one exact 3-column by 2-row grid of SIX equal square SEAMLESS MATERIAL SWATCHES, no gutters, no labels, each flat orthographic top-down material with uniform diffuse unbaked lighting. Landscape 1536x1024 composition. Reading order: 1 warm ochre compacted tropical footpath dirt with a few small gravel mineral flecks, 2 warm gray limestone with tiny moss patches and restrained cracks, 3 aged brown timber straight parallel planks from an Indonesian fishing pier with subtle grain, 4 woven natural bamboo wall mat with tight diagonal crossing strips, 5 dense dried golden brown rumbia thatch roofing in horizontal overlapping bundles, 6 dense clustered rich green small tropical leaf surface. Strong crafted PIXEL ART, crisp square clusters in a shared roughly 128-pixel source detail per swatch, restrained 24-color earthy ochre, brown, limestone gray, fern green palette. These will be wrapped onto REAL 3D meshes and lit dynamically: absolutely NO perspective, NO side faces, NO ambient baked shadow, NO lighting gradient, NO border/frame, NO text, NO whole buildings, NO isolated rocks, NO recognizable symbols or sacred motifs, NO copied commercial assets. All six entire square cells filled edge to edge with texture, tileable within each cell. High visual consistency, no glossy plastic or blurry painted photorealism.

## Prompt vegetasi

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original transparent foliage cutout atlas for Indonesian maritime pixel art game Jalasmara. Exactly 3 columns by 2 rows with six equal cells, transparent RGBA background and ample clear padding inside each cell so shapes NEVER cross cell boundaries. Reading order: 1 large lush overhead oblique three-quarter tropical broadleaf tree crown, 2 overhead oblique coconut palm crown with long curved green fronds radiating around a subtle center, 3 fan of fern fronds from small bottom-center base, 4 tall clump of broad banana leaves with short visible central stalk at bottom center, 5 dense small tropical coastal shrub with individual green leaf clumps, 6 trailing epiphytic vines hanging down from upper edge. Original Indonesian coastal rainforest atmosphere. Crafted detailed pixel art square clusters, same earthy forest/olive greens, golden green edges, cool teal underside palette; clearly separated silhouettes and real transparent gaps between palm fronds and fern leaves. Three-quarter viewpoint, soft neutral diffuse unbaked light, no ground plane, no earth islands, no pots, no background/checkerboard, no outlines/frames/labels, no characters or sacred motifs, no copied commercial assets. Each entire subject completely visible, all cells separated by transparent negative space. Landscape atlas.
```
