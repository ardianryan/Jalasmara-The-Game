# Tamansari pipe-state cinematic plates

These two original Jala Smara environment variants show the same Pelataran Tamansari shot after the Phase 1 Gajah Mina pipe neutralization and after its remaining outlet is sealed. They are **candidate cutscene backgrounds**, not navigable geometry or proof of an integrated gameplay sequence. The boss, Nala, particles, and title/UI remain separate runtime layers. Ardian Ryan supplied the screenplay and visual direction; a Codex agent prompted and curated the edits on 16 November 2026.

Keep the original cheerful teal/amber comfy lighting and sharp square pixel clusters; no airbrush, no photorealism, no new characters, no boss, no people, no UI, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no extra floating pipes. The requested result is a static environmental variant of this SAME shot.
