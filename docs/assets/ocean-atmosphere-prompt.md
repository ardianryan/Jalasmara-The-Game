# Atlas atmosfer laut

Dibuat melalui built-in imagegen pada 8 November 2026, keluaran asli 1536 × 1024 dengan alpha. Disimpan sebagai `client-godot/resources/art/ocean-atmosphere-atlas.png`. Referensi pertama hanya mengarahkan komposisi kedalaman; referensi kedua adalah atlas tekstur milik proyek. Tidak ada screenshot referensi yang dikemas ke permainan.

## Prompt produksi

Use case: stylized-concept. Production bitmap asset atlas for Jalasmara original Indonesian maritime pixel art exploration game. Image 1 is ONLY a reference for geological depth, layered underwater canyon composition, NEVER copy its game art, UI or characters. Image 2 is our existing authored rock palette/texture reference. Create ONE clean transparent sprite atlas, landscape 1536x1024 pixels, EXACTLY 3 columns by 2 rows of equal 512x512 cells, no margins labels lines text UI or background water. SIX DISTINCT isolated environment cutouts, one per cell, entirely contained with 24pixel padding, transparent background all around; baseline at 95 percent cell. Top row left: broad massive weathered porous limestone wall with stepped overhangs and a natural cleft, looks volumetric and quarter-view with top planes and shadowed sides. Top row middle: tall slender karst rock pillar with jagged rounded broken top and layered ledges. Top row right: ruined ancient FICTIONAL Nusantara submerged plain split stone gateway with modest stepped masonry and fallen blocks, no deity faces no sacred symbols no lettering no specific real monument. Bottom row left: dense marine seagrass and long seaweed fronds growing on a small stone base, organic varied silhouettes. Bottom row middle: broken limestone arch and fallen sediment blocks with a dark open hole that stays truly transparent. Bottom row right: asymmetrical branching staghorn coral, sea fans and shelf corals on an eroded rock edge, muted teal/moss/ochre colors. Cohesive detailed hand placed pixel art look, crisp visible square pixel clusters, no smooth vector outlines, no blur no photorealism no plastic low-poly shading. Rich convincing material textures matching image2, but midrange subdued contrast and teal green slate palette, pale limestone highlights. Orthographic front-three-quarter side-view, each object has visible thickness and top planes. These will be tinted and hazed dynamically at different distances in engine, so don't bake broad glow, rays, atmospheric background, water, fish, characters, floor planes, cast shadows outside sprites, lighting bloom or outlines. Entire outside of each silhouette fully transparent. Distinct organic shapes, avoid mechanical repetition. Do not reproduce anything from reference image1, only use its layering principle.

## Susunan dan pemeriksaan

Kisi 3 × 2, sel 512 × 512: dinding kapur, pilar karst, gerbang fiksi, vegetasi laut, lengkung batu, karang tepi. Texel latar memiliki alpha 0–4/255; shader membuang alpha rendah agar tidak muncul bidang persegi. Bentuk padat umumnya 247–253/255. Kabut, pengurangan saturasi dan kontras, serta pergeseran kamera ditentukan engine per lapisan; tidak dibakar ke latar.

Gerbang adalah desain fiksi dunia Jalasmara tanpa klaim sebagai replika monumen atau lambang sakral. Nama dan arah budaya mengikuti [pedoman budaya](../brand/cultural-direction.md).
