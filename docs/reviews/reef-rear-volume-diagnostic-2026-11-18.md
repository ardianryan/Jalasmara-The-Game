# Reef 12 rear-volume and face-facet diagnostic — 18 November 2026

Status: **both visual candidates rejected; no runtime or test source retained**. Branch `work/reef-volume-parity` started at local `main` `9b7ce47`. The goal was to remove the dark, horizontal slab immediately below the valve in the deep 667×375 and 844×390 ocean views without changing server-owned terrain, the six boss resonance positions, coral mounts, or collision.

The unchanged [667×375 baseline](../visual/ocean-reef12-diagnostic-20261118/baseline-deep-667x375.png) shows the defect: a near-horizontal dark edge at roughly screen y=188 continuing to the right and a broad opaque face down to roughly y=291. The original diagnostic had already identified `ReefRelief12` as the node. This pass used the same `scripts/tests/coral-rock-render.gd` native fixture, `balanced` quality, ocean camera and resource state. The first candidate varied the rear Z depth of the existing 13 contour vertices between 0.55 and 0.94 times the original extrusion. It used the same mesh, triangles, material, atlas and `WORLD.terrainContours` source. The [rear-depth frame](../visual/reef-volume-diagnostic-20261118/rejected-rear-667x375.png) still has the straight upper edge and slab. The second candidate divided only reef 12's terrace quads into three same-mesh facets with restrained edge tint, retaining the rear-depth trial. It also passed the focused fixture, but the [667×375](../visual/reef-volume-diagnostic-20261118/rejected-front-facet-667x375.png) and [844×390](../visual/reef-volume-diagnostic-20261118/rejected-front-facet-844x390.png) frames still read as the same slab. Against the baseline, only 478 of 12,894 pixels in the central upper slab crop `(360,188)–(667,230)` changed in the second frame; this is far too small to establish an art improvement. No candidate is recommended for integration.

The source explains the failure. `HybridOcean.gd` scales the 3D root's Y by `1/cos(12°)` and places its orthographic camera at the corresponding 12° tilt. `TerrainGeometry._point(at, depth)` sets Y to `-at.y + sin(12°)*depth`. The camera's vertical projection is therefore `cos(12°) * Y / cos(12°) - sin(12°) * depth = -at.y`: **Z extrusion is canceled in screen-space position by design**. Changing rear depth can alter light and overlap, but it cannot move the slab silhouette. The broad top and side are produced by `_build_reef` from the 1,844×160-world-unit terrain-12 polygon and its inset `front` polygon. The `Geometry2D.intersect_polygons(front, strip)` top-fill pass and the `outline→front` terrace pass both inherit the long, sparsely sampled XY edges. The bad upper line is thus a projected XY boundary in `ReefRelief12`, not a shadow, UV seam or missing Z-depth facet. The terrain mesh renders in `HybridOcean`'s own 3D `SubViewport`, behind the living-water CanvasLayer and 2D actors, then receives the existing ComfyGrade on its display TextureRect. Coral atlas props and the valve sit over the same rock view; they cannot mask a full-width line.

Renderer-only subdivision of the current top contour would add vertices along the *same straight segment* and leave the silhouette unchanged. Moving those visual-only XY vertices enough to scallop the edge would expose water where the server still blocks Nala, or draw rock over a server-passable lane. A tiny internal surface facet can improve texture without altering collision, but both this and the earlier lip trial were visually insufficient at actual game size. The next viable package should author a smoother **shared** terrain-12 XY outline and matching server collision in `packages/shared/src/world.ts`, using broad eroded arcs rather than sharp alternating peaks; keep the existing valve and six boss points with tested route clearance. The renderer should then add bounded rock-face value variation inside that outline. Such a shared-world package requires a separate owner, map/navigation tests, and native 667×375/844×390 plus browser gameplay review. It is not safe to relabel this renderer-only experiment as a fix.
