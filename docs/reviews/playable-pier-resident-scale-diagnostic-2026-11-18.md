# Playable pier resident scale diagnostic — 18 November 2026

Status: source and existing original-size browser evidence reviewed on branch `work/pier-resident-scale-20261118` at base `16190a2f8662138f5a25f17403bc5da9898a0571`. **No runtime change is accepted by this report.** This is not a new native moving capture or physical-device sign-off.

The [844×390 ready hub frame](../visual/moodboard-staging-20261118/hub-ready-844x390.webp) puts Nala and the nearby pier resident at roughly 55–60 visible pixels each, approximately 14–15% of the viewport height. Their feet have separate support points on the deck. The [667×375 forest frame](../visual/moodboard-staging-20261118/forest-canopy-667x375.webp) likewise shows the player around 60 visible pixels high. These are approximate visual measurements from existing released browser captures, not a measured moving sequence. They do not establish that every resident's eight-facing art reads well.

The apparent smallness comes chiefly from the broad dock and surrounding HUD composition, plus limited identity detail at this pixel budget. `LandActors3D.gd` already uses nearest texture sampling; it gives Nala a 100-world-unit land body and draws the original resident rig at `CharacterVisual.LAND_SIZE / 112 × bodyScale`. The director is 1.0 and children are deliberately 0.62–0.70 of adult size. `VillageResidentBody3D.gd` uses that same scale for thigh, shin, hip, hand prop and foot-contact geometry. Multiplying only the rendered node would disconnect the actual emitted soles from the authoritative support. Multiplying all body parts would change child/adult proportions relative to Nala and enlarge the same source pixels without adding identity. A universal 1.1–1.2 scale adjustment is therefore **rejected** until matched moving evidence demonstrates a net gain.

Recommended next authoring task: improve selected resident source-pixel silhouettes or directional poses and inspect them in the real dock camera beside Nala. A separate camera/HUD composition pass can reclaim space without changing body/world scale. That work needs explicit ownership of resident art/rig or camera/HUD files; it is outside this bounded renderer-only lane. Before acceptance, record matched 667×375 and 844×390 native moving dock captures at the same daylight/weather, eight facings, two gait cycles, idle/dialog stop/resume, roof/deck occlusion, soles and frame pacing. Reuse the project-owned source art; moodboard pixels are reference only. The existing stills do not meet that gate.

No Godot import, native render, browser run, typecheck, test, build, push or staging action was performed for this diagnostic. Tools used: `git`, `rg`, source reading, and image viewing. No new asset or third-party license was introduced.
