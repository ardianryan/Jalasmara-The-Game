# Phase 1 cutscene actor follow-up: garden and victory

Read-only audit on `work/cutscene-beat-audit-20261118`, based on main `3bc8704`. This audit used existing project screenshots at their original 667×375 and 844×390 sizes and inspected `client-godot/scripts/CinematicActionLayer.gd`. It made no runtime or art changes and did not reserve the native renderer. The accepted Ki Lurah prologue close pose is outside this scope.

The current ensemble sizes are broadly appropriate for their depth. Enlarging every citizen would obscure the pier or garden and compete with the dialogue portrait. The remaining visible gap is that the authored action is clearer than the acting that causes it:

1. **Garden crisis, Gito's three marble shots.** [Garden at 667×375](../visual/cutscene-actor-idle-20261118/garden_children-late-667x375.webp) shows Gito readable on the left, but the matching [early frame](../visual/cutscene-actor-idle-20261118/garden_children-early-667x375.webp) differs only subtly in the children. In `_draw_garden`, marbles launch from a fixed point above Gito while his `_actor` call has only the generic small lean; there is no sling/throw release in his silhouette. Add a bounded, source-pixel arm or sling pose sequence timed to the three existing launches. Keep his feet on the original garden plane and the guard impacts at their current coordinates. Preserve the other four children's current size and the dialogue/skip clearance. A held throwing pose, short release, and recovery should be legible without displacing or shearing limbs.

2. **Victory pier, children's cheering.** Compare [t0](../visual/cutscene-focal-stage-20261118/victory-03-t0-667x375.webp), [t1](../visual/cutscene-focal-stage-20261118/victory-03-t1-667x375.webp), [t2](../visual/cutscene-focal-stage-20261118/victory-03-t2-667x375.webp), and [t2 at 844×390](../visual/cutscene-focal-stage-20261118/victory-03-t2-844x390.webp). Nala and Beno have a clear focal hierarchy, but the five children on the dock read as nearly still extras. `_draw_victory` applies only an absolute-sine hop of up to three scene units to each whole sprite, with no visible cheer gesture. Give one or two near children staggered, pixel-bounded raised-arm or turn poses and a short hop that returns to a planted pier contact point. Keep the remaining children at support scale, the sampan berth and Nala's tablet visible, and the bottom dialogue clear. Avoid making all five bounce in lockstep.

For both changes, owner is `CinematicActionLayer.gd` plus any new project-owned pose source. Acceptance requires a source-pinned moving capture and original-size t0/t1/t2 frames at **both** 667×375 and 844×390: the gesture must be recognizable at game size, feet must return to the painted plane, and reduced-motion mode must use a stable readable pose. Verify dialogue and skip controls remain unobstructed. These stills diagnose staging; they do not prove browser motion, device performance, or full moodboard parity.

Tools actually used: `rg`, `sed`, and image inspection of already-curated project evidence. No new asset, upstream dependency, license, Godot run, or browser run was introduced.
