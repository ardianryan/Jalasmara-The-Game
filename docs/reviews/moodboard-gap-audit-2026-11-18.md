# Moodboard gap audit — 18 November 2026

Status: read-only review of source `659dfdfe5f60787c5fd81ed2ca966183f64db120`, not visual acceptance or a release. The target is the owner's cheerful, crisp, warm-and-cool 3/4 coastal world and expressive pixel cutscenes. The supplied moodboard is reference only; its pixels must not enter the game. The latest [parity report](moodboard-parity-followup-2026-11-18.md) already tracks the two largest blockers: broad angular land routes and Reef 12's long right ledge.
