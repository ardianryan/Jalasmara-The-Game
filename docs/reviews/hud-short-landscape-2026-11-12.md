# Short-landscape HUD visibility — 12 November 2026

The prior 844×390 browser capture showed a 128-pixel minimap centered over the route and a 420×120 status panel covering the diver and oxygen gauge. This package changes the existing Godot HUD layout without changing controls, the map data, player state, or gameplay rules.

On physical landscape viewports no taller than 480 pixels, the minimap rim now renders at approximately 80 physical pixels; the full 128-pixel version remains at 960×540. Status messages size to their actual text, use readable Nunito, and sit above the minimap. During a dive they anchor to the right of the oxygen/depth instrument. The action and net touch targets retain their previous size. A focused regression measures minimap size, viewport bounds, status/minimap clearance, dive instrument clearance, and touch target size at 844×390 desktop pointer, 667×375 touch, and 960×540 desktop. The test is part of `npm run check:world`.
