# Coastal life and material integration — 15 November 2026

The owner asks for a colorful, comfortable pixel world at the real game viewport, continuously changing light, and vegetation that responds to wind. This package is a bounded visual improvement on the existing Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility renderer; it does not replace the terrain, collision map, server-owned clock, game engine or source art.

## Accepted source

- Rooted coral clusters now pivot at their visible base and respond to current in a small, quantized arc. The [coral motion report](coral-current-motion-2026-11-15.md) and [three native frames](../visual/coral-current-still-667x375-20261115.png) record the original atlas, 43-cluster fixture, reduced-motion behavior and actual-size movement.
- The existing dock shader now gives long boards sparse staggered end joints. Its [matched 844×390 screenshots](moodboard-dock-plank-joints-2026-11-15.md) show the final lower-contrast version; an overly regular first trial was discarded.
- The existing foliage atlas supplies a second, slightly narrower crown for alternating mangroves and a few broadleaf trees. [Forest](../visual/moodboard-foliage-forest-ab-20261115.png) and [hub](../visual/moodboard-foliage-hub-ab-20261115.png) day/dusk A/B sheets show the changed silhouette at 667×375 and 844×390. Pixel-stepped sway now respects signed `wind_x`. The [foliage report](moodboard-foliage-composition-2026-11-15.md) records 16 native frames and focused checks.
- Surface ferns, crop heads and small flowers now join the wind response; shared GPU material keeps the crop/flower heads in the existing batch and pins their lower vertices. The [land wind report](land-wind-coverage-2026-11-15.md) and [actual-size hub/forest review](../visual/land-wind-hub-667x375-20261115.webp) cover signed wind, calm/reduced motion and native rendering.
- The existing underwater valve and broken leak sprites have been moved onto their respective reef shelves without changing server anchors or collision. The [grounding report](ocean-pipe-grounding-2026-11-15.md) and [667×375 native A/B sheet](../visual/ocean-pipe-grounding-667x375-20261115.png) document the visual correction; the pipe is no longer floating in that tested camera view.
- Small original purple rings now move in the dock water and rise from the grounded underwater valve. Their visual state follows authoritative valve and boss progress, so repair weakens the plume and purification removes it after reload. The [effect report](pollution-bubbles-2026-11-15.md) and [native 667×375 before/after](../visual/pollution-bubbles/underwater-before-667x375.png) document fixed 18/12 instance budgets, reduced motion, water-only placement and the final no-leak render fixture.
- Thirty-nine of 43 coral roots are seated into the visible reef shelf with small stone contacts batched by existing material. [Shallow/deep native A/B at 667×375](../visual/coral-rock-integration-667x375-20261116.webp) and the [coral-rock report](coral-rock-integration-2026-11-16.md) show the bounded improvement; four side-edge clusters keep their earlier anchor and still need moving contact review.

Thirteen code-drawn transparent [future props](future-world-prop-assets-2026-11-15.md) are also prepared with editable source, a hash manifest and [contact sheet](../visual/future-world-props-v1-20261115.png). They cover distinct corporate power props, Saras's field equipment and later northern forest states. They are **unplaced** and cannot be inferred to appear in the Phase 1 game or staging; the forest fire/plantation remains future-only.

The coastline outcrop, authored headland and coordinated reef recoloring trials did **not** enter the runtime. Their A/B evidence showed an imperceptible outcrop, a stone shoulder abruptly cut at the dock, or flattened/noisy reef shapes. See the [outcrop](moodboard-coastline-outcrop-trial-2026-11-15.md), [headland](moodboard-authored-coastline-trial-2026-11-15.md), and [reef](moodboard-reef-coordinated-pixel-grid-trial-2026-11-15.md) reports. A [depth-gradient shader experiment](ocean-parallax-coherence-audit-2026-11-16.md) was rejected because it darkened deeper water without clearly improving reef separation. Shrinking rear rock cards was also [rejected after actual-size comparison](ocean-depth-composition-trial-2026-11-16.md): it widened the empty teal lane, and an initial candidate capture unexpectedly omitted Nala before a diagnostic repeat showed her again. Neither candidate changed the runtime. Keeping rejected geometry and palette out of the game is important to the natural island edge and readable underwater silhouettes.
