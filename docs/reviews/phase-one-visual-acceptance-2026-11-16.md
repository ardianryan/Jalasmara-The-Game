## Corrections required for visual sign-off

1. **Pipe geometry and state, scenes 4.2–4.3.** In `boss_phase_two-01-844x390.png` the upper-right painted pipe remains intact while a small burst appears to its left. `CinematicActionLayer.gd:104-120` fixes its break origin at `(0.77W, 0.22H)`, apart from the painted outlet. In `victory-02-844x390.png`, the drawn stone/tablet at roughly `(0.52W, 0.46H)` never reaches the pipe; `CinematicActionLayer.gd:579-589` keeps it there for the whole beat. Register the outlet position once per plate, draw the break *on* that outlet, then render a cap/stone that visibly travels into and covers it. Check the start, contact and final frames at both sizes. Keep the actual server boss and six-resonance rule unchanged.
2. **Actor grounding and scale, scenes 2.2 and 4.5.** In `saras_visit-02-667x375.png`, Saras occupies the Kedai window rail (`CinematicActionLayer.gd:341-349`). In `epilogue-01-844x390.png`, Raka and his two guards are placed at `0.54H`, visibly above the floor, while the crowd uses varying `0.60–0.65H` feet positions (`CinematicActionLayer.gd:615-649`). Place feet along the painted floor perspective and size the foreground actors/tables consistently; capture both landscape sizes after the change.
3. **Readable corrupted-to-healed state, scenes 4.1–4.3.** `boss_intro-02-667x375.png` and `victory-01-667x375.png` both read primarily as a green/teal creature. `CutsceneFrame.gd:170-178,374-375` swaps source atlases, but the actual-size screenshots do not communicate the requested angry red eye → calm blue eye and purple corrosion clearing strongly enough. Make these features legible on the *visible face and pipe*, then verify a side-by-side native capture. This is a visual clarity request, not a claim that no atlas swap exists.

The following are **screenplay-fidelity gaps, not blockers to the existing 3+3-resonance gameplay**: add Nala/Wira's return exchange in 0.3; make the first tasting an actual Ranto close-up and burst; distinguish the guards' threat in 2.1; decide explicitly whether 3.2 remains a morning adaptation or the requested dusk beat; show the surface-clearing transition and Bagas's map-room UV participation. These can use the existing Godot action layer, approved atlases, plates and shaders. Cinetic did not produce the current cutscenes and should not be credited as used.

**Acceptance evidence still needed:** a short temporal capture of the pipe crack, seal, healed-face change, tasting and ending (not only one still per beat); an uninterrupted disposable-account journey through the server milestones; audible BGM/SFX checks; WebGL and device frame pacing. The native capture's automated bounds, ≥16 px dialogue type and ≥48 px targets are useful layout checks (`scripts/tests/phase-one-director-capture.gd:40-57`), but they do not establish motion quality or moodboard parity.
