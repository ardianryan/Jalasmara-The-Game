# Village gait and Gajah Mina motion — 12 November 2026

## Scope and status

The owner approved a bounded motion pass: seven static-looking residents now alternate four simple step poses as they actually travel; Gajah Mina swims within the existing right-side arena and telegraphs attacks from its displayed position. The 480×270 land world budget, 3D terrain, six fixed resonance points, six-success boss rule, rewards, damage and server-owned fight validation remain unchanged. This package was authored on `work/village-boss-motion` from `69eb9e6`. The branch is a candidate until CI and release are recorded; the then-active staging source was `acd70c3` with PCK `38c5aaa95cef`.

## Implementation

- `tools/npc-rig/generate_simple_steps.py` derives the original idle plus four small alternating foot poses for Ki Lurah, Mbok Ratih, Kang Tedi, Saras, Mbah Wira, Beno and Inspektur Raka. The 64-pixel source cells, source hashes and packed strips are under `resources/art/npc-simple-steps-v1`. At runtime, Ki Lurah, Mbok Ratih, Kang Tedi and Saras already have a richer articulated `ResidentArtRig3D`; that rig stays authoritative. `NpcSimpleSteps.gd` drives the three portrait-only residents from distance actually travelled and returns them to idle at rest. The first four strips are fallback art if their richer rig cannot be configured; no rig was downgraded.
- `HubActor.gd` no longer stops route movement simply because Nala is nearby. Dialogue and the shared clock pause still stop actors. The established schedule and job gestures continue.
- `tools/boss-motion/generate_boss_frames.py` cuts the original transparent boss into connected body/head, fin and tail layers, producing eight 192×128 pixel frames. `BossMotion.gd` shares one patrol position, phase, attack clock and origin with the visual in `OceanActors.gd`/`Entities.gd` and projectiles in `Main.gd`. The motion eases through the phase-one/phase-two change and freezes for quiz or host pause. A 0.7-second arc/line warning appears before each attack; the phase-two second projectile also originates at the boss. Recovered mode returns to a calm, slower pose. There is no moving boss-body collision, consistent with the unchanged server contract.
- The authoring guide now recommends 16×16 for tiny details, 32×32 for props and up to 64×64 for characters or large parts, judged at displayed size. No building or terrain retile and no viewport upscaling were attempted.

These are native fixture images, not screenshots of staging. Full uncropped frames remain locally in ignored `artifacts/godot/boss-motion/` and `artifacts/godot/npc-simple-steps/`.

The focused `village-boss-motion.gd` check exercises seven pose strips, rest/movement, both patrol phases, telegraph and projectile origins in `Main`, pause, recovery, session reset and the six-point rule. `village-activities.gd` and `village-routine06.gd` now assert that proximity alone does not interrupt movement. Native fixtures `boss-motion-render.gd` and `npc-simple-steps-render.gd` completed without script errors. The complete `npm run check:world`, `npm run typecheck` and `npm run build` passed with no `SCRIPT ERROR` or `ERROR:` in their logs. The new Web PCK is 69,980,732 bytes, 205,184 bytes above the 69,775,548-byte active baseline. A first world run could not locate `godot` on PATH; rerunning with the project-pinned `GODOT_BIN` is the valid result. The local `npm test` API suite could not start without MongoDB on 27018 and Redis on 6380; no result from that attempt is counted as a pass. GitHub CI with isolated services remains required.

No Windows or Samsung S23 GPU benchmark was available in this worktree. Before staging release, compare visual motion, frame pacing and uninterrupted audio on those physical devices with the current build. The native contact sheet supports a deliberately simple gait only; it does not prove fully natural eight-direction walking. The fixed boss quiz targets remain reachable by design and focused coordinates, but a full six-hit human playthrough on staging remains an acceptance gate.
