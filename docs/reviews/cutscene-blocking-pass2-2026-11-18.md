# Phase 1 cinematic blocking, second pass — 18 November 2026

This source candidate follows the wide-shot scale and victory dock correction on `main` (`52bd836`). It audits 16 representative screenplay beats at the game's actual 667×375 and 844×390 sizes: prologue 1, 4, 6 and 9; first dive 1; first Kedai 1; first service 1; garden crisis 2; Saras visit 2; cave warning 2; earthquake 1; boss intro 1; boss phase two 1; victory 1 and 3; and epilogue 4. The capture fixture saves three motion phases per beat. Other dialogue beats remain covered by its existing focal-character checks.

The two weakest selected beats were the Laskar Kognisi confrontation and the Balai Adat earthquake council. In the garden, the five children held one pose while two security guards stood still despite the flying marbles. The guards now enter over a short, bounded distance using their existing step frames and settle before the hits; the children make staggered, small rallies on their original floor marks. In the council, the adults now converge on the map over less than a second, show existing walking frames while moving, and settle on the same marks. Purple disturbance around the map has a single decaying pulse instead of five identical pulses overdrawn after every actor. Both changes keep characters above the dialogue box and use the project's own sprite sheets and pixel drawing code.

This is an incremental staging correction, not a claim that all screenplay visuals or character motion match the moodboard. The blocking changes are deliberately small; figures remain small next to the painted background at 667×375. The beat-2 garden and beat-1 council A/B show foot contact and no dialogue overlap, while the rest of the screenplay still needs richer character animation and art direction. This package does not change dialogue, story gates, rewards, collision, or equipment. Real browser playback on Windows and Android remains an acceptance step.
