# Authored coastline landform trial rejected

Date: 15 November 2026. Branch: `work/coastline-redesign-20261115`, based on `f1c5ad0`. This is a rejected visual experiment. The commit contains only this report and a curated A/B image; no runtime, map, collision, stair, or dock change is offered for integration.

The [previous outcrop trial](moodboard-coastline-outcrop-trial-2026-11-15.md) was too small to read at game size. I tried broader, location-authored outward landforms on the hub lower coast and both forest riverbanks. The trial kept the authoritative world polygons, walkability, connector points, stairs, and deck geometry unchanged. Three hub shapes were rendered in sequence; the final version used one broad headland with a smooth numerical taper near the dock. It changed 4.77% of the 667×375 hub daylight frame (RGB-channel difference above 8), while the forest west and east views changed 1.37% and 1.47%. Earlier hub variants changed 5.72% and 4.57% but looked even more like a bolted-on stone shoulder.

The [matched 667×375 daylight contact sheet](../visual/coastline-authored-landform-rejected-20261115.webp) compares the final candidate with the original at the same camera and time. Its SHA-256 is `0facb9897c23f85be008c0fb7173b1519bbcf052a8c906a28bb84f96149bb5cc`. I also reviewed a matched 844×390 dusk sheet in the isolated worktree. The forest banks gain a modest readable bend, but the hub headland becomes a large stone mass that appears to end abruptly at the deck. That defect fails the requested continuous, natural island edge. Numeric taper and a higher changed-pixel count do not make the visible join convincing, so the entire runtime trial was reverted.

The trial's focused coastline and natural-terrain-edge tests passed. The latter retained eight surfaces, 512 active nodes, 516 exclusions, and 251 supported nodes. Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility rendered 12 baseline and 12 final frames across three camera positions, two display sizes (667×375 and 844×390), and day/dusk without `SCRIPT ERROR` or `ERROR`. The fixture checked one player, foot error below 0.01, and unchanged world JSON; the world fixture SHA-256 was `d709f37758406c3a8dd27b381b9c78ddea26d967e9475df8efadb717e033ea5a`. The first editor import emitted transient font import errors; a second import was clean before the render. These are native still-frame and source checks, not browser traversal, collision playtesting, GPU/FPS measurement, or Windows/Samsung S23 validation. No staging deployment occurred.

The next coastline attempt should compose the island edge and dock landing together in screen space, with grass cap and limestone strata continuing into a deliberately designed landing. It needs visibly varied silhouettes on both sides of the dock without a separate-looking wedge or clipping the route. Review at 667×375 before expanding the shape across the map; keep the server-owned footprint and existing walkability contract intact.

Tools actually used: isolated Git worktree, project GDScript source/fixture, Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility native renderer, Python Pillow for matched contact sheets and pixel differences, and a local Shapely geometry diagnostic. No third-party art, source code, generated asset, or new license entered the game. The trial code and render fixture were removed before this documentation-only commit.
