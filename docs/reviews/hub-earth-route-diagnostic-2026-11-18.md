# Hub eastern earth route — rejected composition trial

**Decision:** retain the existing hub paint. This trial started on isolated branch `work/hub-earth-route-20261118` from `264d9a8` and changed only a temporary implementation of `joinedSpine()` in `packages/shared/src/village-ground.ts`. The source change was reverted before this diagnostic commit. The current server route, walkable floor, steps, collider, buildings, weather, and render materials remain as they were.

The remaining projected V/Y is not the central stair spur that was already removed. Its right orange arm is the eastern section of the existing lower-court dirt ribbon from `(1370,950)` to `(1600,950)`; the house/dock approach joins it through `(1120,1000)`. At 3/4 camera angle the otherwise shallow graph bend becomes a long bright diagonal. The left upper-court arm is produced separately by route segment paint. Both arms need one coherent site composition, not an edge-only treatment.

I tried two revisions of the entire lower ribbon. The first rounded corners over a larger radius and tapered the eastern visual width from 72 to 48 world units. It softened the edges but left the orange V. The second authored a gentle ±11-unit meander on the eastern branch and reduced its visual width from 72 toward 30 world units, while retaining the original route points in the dirt paint and preserving the full authoritative route. At the original 667×375 and 844×390 sizes, the right arm looked narrower, yet the V/Y silhouette still dominated and the branch appeared to pinch without fixing the larger composition. Neither revision met the art acceptance gate.

The focused `village-ground.test.ts` suite passed 8/8 and `npm run typecheck` passed on both source revisions. The actual route centers `(1370,950)`, `(1430,950)`, `(1490,950)`, `(1545,950)`, and `(1600,950)` remained dirt and walkable in CPU queries. A cold Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility editor import logged temporary missing font cache resources before completing; native baseline and both candidate six-frame fixtures passed, with no `SCRIPT ERROR` or `ERROR:` in the runtime capture logs. Baseline and candidate used the same daytime camera positions from `scripts/tests/terrain-facets-render.gd`. No full world suite, browser traversal, device measurement, CI, or staging was run on the rejected visual source. Full ignored captures/logs are under `artifacts/godot/hub-earth-route/` and `.runtime/hub-earth-route/` in the disposable worktree.

The next redesign needs to compose the upper-court approach, lower house/dock forecourt, central stairs, and eastern destination as one activity site. A simple taper, color adjustment, stone overlay, or edge jitter has now failed at actual size. Before changing any route graph, verify where residents and the player actually travel; the visual route cannot lead to a dead end or have NPC feet routinely off the path. This remains an open visual acceptance problem, not a shipped parity fix.

Evidence is project-owned Godot imagery only. Lossless A/B sheets were assembled with Pillow 11.3.0; no moodboard image, external art, new runtime tool, or license was added.
