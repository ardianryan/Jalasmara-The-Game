This experiment confirms that a few tapered polygons over the existing grass and material cannot produce the natural, interlocked path and terrain shown in the moodboard.

The two defects need separate treatment. **Color/material:** the current `dirt` shader and texture remain high-key yellow-orange at game size even when the route is narrower, so a locally graded soil palette or mixed grass/soil atlas is needed; a uniformly darker second band failed in the preceding trial. **Shape/composition:** the existing stone service paving and house footprint clip individual dirt pieces into thin exposed strokes, while a free-standing junction polygon creates a V. The next design must explicitly author how soil meets that stone paving, the house foundation, the stair landing and the dock threshold, with grass or small stones occupying the leftover gaps rather than simply widening dirt.

The next pass needs an authored **area-level** path/grass/stone composition rather than another centerline paint adjustment. Design the house approach, stair landing and dock entrance together with local ground material transitions and removal of clipped remnants, then compare original-size render and actual movement. Avoid broad union, two-tone bands, alpha fringe expansion and isolated tapered ribbons already rejected in this series.
