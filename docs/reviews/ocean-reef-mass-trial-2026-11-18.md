# Sanctuary reef mass trials — 18 November 2026

**Decision: rejected; no runtime source change.** This worktree started at `main` `0b00f67`. The accepted `ReefRelief12` still has a long horizontal shelf and dark underside in the native deep view. The previous small crowns and scallops had been visually imperceptible, so this pass tested a structurally different shared contour. Both trials used project-owned atlas and Godot meshes, with server collision and renderer reading the same `WORLD.terrainContours[12]`; no reference image was distributed.

An 800×390 world-unit rock mass replaced the original 1,844×160 shelf and retained a clear valve approach and all six boss resonance points. Its 18-point contour was simple, inside its rectangle and clear of the boss. `terrain-layers.gd` passed 15 contours, 523 terrace bands and 5,256 projected vertices. The full native `coral-rock-render.gd` scene, however, produced **no first shallow frame in about 75 seconds** while its Godot process consumed roughly 13% CPU. It logged no script error, but verification was incomplete; I terminated that process and rejected the candidate. This does not prove the precise stall cause.

A compact 500×160 outcrop then shortened the shelf without moving the valve or resonance points. Source checks and `npm run typecheck` passed. Godot's `terrain-layers.gd` passed 15 contours, 521 bands and 5,184 projected vertices; `ocean-platform06.gd` passed. The native full-ocean fixture completed at 667×375 and 844×390 with no `ERROR:` or `SCRIPT ERROR`, 43 coral pivots, 39 mounts, oxygen 77 and coins 700 unchanged. The [667×375 A/B](../visual/ocean-reef-mass-trial-20261118/compact-outcrop-ab-667x375.webp) and [844×390 A/B](../visual/ocean-reef-mass-trial-20261118/compact-outcrop-ab-844x390.webp) show why this is rejected: shortening the ledge exposes the autogenerated rear support as a straight, narrow mushroom stalk with a harsh geometric underside. That is farther from the requested joined 2.5D reef than the accepted image. The A/B files are lossless development evidence, baseline left and trial right.

Future work needs to author the **reef volume and its rear buttress as one composition** at actual screen size, rather than shortening the foreground shelf while keeping the generic root generator. It should verify the valve, all boss targets, coral grounding, collision and render contour together, then capture both native sizes and browser movement. These stills do not establish Windows/Samsung S23 performance or full moodboard parity. All trial source changes were restored before this report; Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility, Node.js 26 and ImageMagick were the tools used.
