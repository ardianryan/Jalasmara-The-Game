# Phase 1 dialogue portrait fallback, 16 November 2026

The small-landscape cutscene fixture had no `surface_hub.residents` in `GameState.world`. `DialoguePresenter` therefore returned no portrait for Ki Lurah, Saras, Gito and Kinasih, leaving only a nameplate and making their dialogue feel detached from the scene. The presenter now resolves the known resident cast to the existing project art even before the world response arrives. A populated world still supplies its resident ID and valid appearance index. The five child portraits use an upper-body crop of their existing transparent 96×128 source frame, so a speaking child reads as a portrait instead of a tiny full-body figure.

This is a dialogue cut-in repair, separate from the in-scene actor scale and choreography pass. It does not add or generate art, alter the story, change server state, or enlarge the dialogue tray. The known resident mapping is local to `DialoguePresenter.gd`; a speaker with no authored portrait remains text-only rather than being misidentified as another character.
