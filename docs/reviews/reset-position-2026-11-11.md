A successful click calls the authenticated, CSRF-protected `/api/adventure/reset-position` endpoint, closes the menu and reloads the game through the existing loading screen. Errors remain visible in the menu. The endpoint selects a connected, walkable anchor in the player's current land area: the hub surface spawn or the forest entry.
