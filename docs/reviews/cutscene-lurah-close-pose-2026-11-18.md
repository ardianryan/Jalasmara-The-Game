# Ki Lurah foreground teaching pose in the prologue harbor

The existing 667×375 [settled harbor lesson](../visual/cutscene-focal-stage-20261118/prologue-01-t2-667x375.webp) left Ki Lurah at roughly the children's visual weight, even after the focal blocking pass. Enlarging the 64×64 walking frame further would have made its sparse pixels dominant. This isolated candidate uses the project's original high-detail Ki Lurah portrait: a 512×512 atlas cell is reduced once to a transparent 128×128, 64-color source-pixel cutout. The composition moves that actor from the far pier to a connected timber foreground tread as the camera settles; the three children remain visible on the older dock. The wide establishing moment is retained.
