# Reef 12 moodboard parity trials — 18 November 2026

**Decision: no runtime change accepted.** This diagnostic started from `main` at `160f525` in `work/ocean-moodboard-fix-20261118`. The accepted Reef 12 shoulder still reads as a long ledge with a dark undercut at the native 667×375 ocean view. It is connected to the porous limestone and coral roots, but it does not yet meet the requested natural 2.5D reef quality. No authored reference or moodboard image was copied into the game.

I tested two deliberately bounded changes. First, five small rock crowns were added inside the authoritative blocked contour to the existing `ReefRelief12` SurfaceTool and material. This added ten triangles and no new mesh, draw call or collider. The native deep 667×375 frame changed about 104 of 250,125 pixels (0.04%) against the accepted Reef12 image, and the long shelf was visually unchanged. This trial was reverted.

Second, I made two broad scallops in the *shared* `WORLD.terrainContours[12]` top edge, so server swim blocking and Godot rendering would remain aligned. The new points stayed inside the original terrain rectangle; the native `terrain-layers.gd` fixture passed 15 contours, 573 bands and 5,598 projected vertices inside the contour. `coral-rock-render.gd` passed shallow and deep views at 667×375 and 844×390, with 43 coral pivots, 39 mounted roots, oxygen 77 and coins 700 unchanged, and no `ERROR:` or `SCRIPT ERROR`. This trial was also reverted. The A/B files are lossless WebP, baseline on the left and trial on the right. They are development evidence, not distributable game assets.

Small ridges and contour tweaks are below the visibility threshold.
