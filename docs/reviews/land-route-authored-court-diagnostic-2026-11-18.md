# Hub court ground composition: rejected native trial

**Status:** visual diagnostic only. No route or ground runtime change is accepted from this trial. The candidate was made in `work/route-area-authored-20261118` from `0b00f67`; the two curated comparisons below are its retained output. The source candidate was reverted before committing this report.

The earlier segment mask made the market path a thin, flat ribbon. This trial instead authored broad irregular dirt footprints around the lower market and upper court, cut grass holes into them, and placed small stone thresholds at stairs and dock. It left the route graph, connectors, elevations, and authoritative collision surfaces untouched. A top-down preview showed distinct courtyard masses, but the actual camera view did not meet the comfy moodboard.

The prior baseline was produced from the same runtime code before this docs-only source commit; it is not a fresh simultaneous render.

CPU inspection found 60 generated decorative surfaces (55 dirt, 5 stone). The authored lower court covered 52.6% of its polygon and upper court 26.4%. One upper-court piece (`village_ground_upper_court_kedai_market_approach_0`) was invalid under Shapely's polygon validity check. This came from clipping a concave authored subject against convex court and exclusion boundaries with a routine that assumes each intermediate remains a simple polygon. It must not be promoted to runtime. `npm run typecheck` passed for the first area revision. The existing focused tests were written for the old 72-unit segment-width contract and failed under the trial; they were deliberately not rewritten for a rejected visual change. Godot's first editor import logged transient missing font import errors; a second import had no `SCRIPT ERROR` or `ERROR`. Native renderer produced the 667 and 844 frames and exited successfully. No browser, Windows, or S23 performance claim follows from this diagnostic.

The next useful experiment needs a **new material composition**, not another polygon-width adjustment. Model small packed-earth patches and stone thresholds as separate bounded shapes near activity anchors (shelter entrance, market stalls, stair foot, dock) instead of connecting them into one long soil field. Retain much more unbroken lawn and a visibly grounded stair-to-stone transition. Sample existing ground texture/lighting in `client-godot/scripts/land/LandMaterials.gd` and the geometry path in `client-godot/scripts/land/LandWorld3D.gd` before changing color; color-only reduction previously failed. Ground shape ownership belongs to `packages/shared/src/village-ground.ts` and focused tests in `apps/api/test/village-ground.test.ts`; terrain material ownership must be assigned separately to prevent two agents editing the same renderer. The old `routePaint` and this trial's broad court mask should not be repeated. Require a source-level valid-polygon check and original-size 667/844 native A/B before accepting any candidate.

The generated Godot export, import cache, render output, and top-down preview remain local diagnostic artifacts and are not part of the commit. The retained A/B images are game screenshots, not distribution art. No third-party asset, tool, or license was added.
