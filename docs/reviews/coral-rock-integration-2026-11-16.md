# Coral contacts on the existing reef

Date: 16 November 2026. Source base: `be58e0b`. This is an isolated **local candidate**, not a GitHub CI artifact or staging release.

The existing coral placement uses the outer terrain contour plus seven world units. The rendered reef top is an inset polygon, so several opaque coral bases appeared to stand in front of the stone edge. `TerrainGeometry.gd` now locates the matching inset shelf and moves eligible roots a bounded distance into it. Each supported cluster gets a low two-facet stone contact in the same nearest-filtered reef material; contacts are batched by the three existing material columns rather than adding one draw call per coral. Coral cards sit at depth `z=50`, just ahead of the existing top relief, while stone contacts reach `z=51` and overlap the opaque base. The original atlas, 43 placements, authored scale, current-driven root rotation, reduced-motion behavior, collision, swim lane, server coordinates, fish and boss targets are unchanged. No new asset or runtime dependency was used.

The first seating attempt left a small deep cluster partly occluded by the valve ledge. Its depth was corrected before final evidence. The final native fixture reports **43 coral pivots, 39 shelf mounts**; four edge placements retain their original anchor because the inset shelf has no valid point at their authored X. They are not given a fabricated free-floating stone. A headless audit located those fallbacks at world `(69.36,1084.45)`, `(3668.81,2017.32)`, `(4353.72,2831.23)` and `(6082.87,2832.34)`. Their individual moving-camera rock contact has not been verified, so they remain an explicit follow-up.
