# Underwater side-view depth audit — no runtime change

The owner requires underwater play to read as a side-view 2.5D scene with coherent parallax and scale, while land keeps its current 3/4 camera. I inspected `OceanBackdrop.gd`, its focused world test, the existing native reef captures, and one new 844×390 native reef baseline with the grounded outlet source temporarily loaded. The baseline is [here](../visual/ocean-parallax-audit-baseline-844x390-20261116.png), SHA-256 `b259b580b3806e8a95bc8d2482d5b4b68155e3c4f6f20d5ea5f8eb907431920f`. The pipe is outside this reef camera, so this frame does **not** prove pipe geometry or plume alignment.

The existing background already uses 48 bounded transparent cards on three spatial planes: far/mid/near parallax factors 0.18/0.42/0.70 horizontally and 0.12/0.28/0.55 vertically. Detail contrast and visibility increase toward the playable foreground; foliage cards stop at deeper water. The camera remains orthographic side-view. The local mismatch visible in the baseline is chiefly the painterly, very large rear rock silhouette beside crisp fish, Nala and coral, plus a wide empty swim lane. A color shader alone cannot repair that asset-scale/composition mismatch.

I tested a *source-only* alternative for the continuous water shader: anchor its teal-to-deep-blue interpolation to world distance below the surface rather than the UV height of its approximately 4,984-unit quad. With the current `smoothstep` and world dimensions, the interpolation at world Y 620 (shallow reef) would move only from about 0.129 to 0.141; at Y 1600 it would jump from about 0.383 to 0.552. That barely helps the common reef view and could make deeper background cards appear detached from much darker water. The candidate was discarded before distribution. The native four-shot fixture was stopped after its first actual-size reef baseline to release the sequential Godot slot for pollution verification; no candidate screenshot, deep-frame comparison, performance benchmark, or physical-device proof is claimed.

No runtime, physics, collision, asset, pipeline, test or gameplay file is committed in this package. The next visual pass should author a smaller rear cliff/reef silhouette and assess it at 667×375 and 844×390 in the actual side-view scene, while keeping foreground targets and the grounded pipe visible. It should retain the three existing depth planes and mobile card budget, and reject any raster that reads as a floating slab or opaque rectangle. No external art/tool was incorporated; this audit used source inspection, project Godot 4.7.2 native rendering and simple world-coordinate calculations. The worktree was based on `ba38462`; the integrator will record the final merged SHA and staging state.
