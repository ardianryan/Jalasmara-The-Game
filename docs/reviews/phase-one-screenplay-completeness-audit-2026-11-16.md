# Phase 1 screenplay and cutscene completeness audit — 16 November 2026

**Finding:** the playable Phase 1 story arc is implemented as an adaptation, but the owner's directed screenplay is **not complete as a cutscene production**. Sixteen numbered scenes are represented by twelve skippable Godot dialogue sequences. Their server-owned milestones, several music and sound cues, three original illustrated plates, atmospheric motion, and three small coded action motifs are present. Most specified actor blocking, prop handoffs, camera choreography, and transformations are still narration rather than rendered action. Do not describe the current staging build as the finished visual screenplay.

Audit baseline: local `main` `f60ed3006ab8ca8f70fff6a905a29817433c9067` (documentation-only successor to the game release); staging game source `e17d539ebcbaa298e146badb71bb9f5bab02dc14`, CI PCK `8eed4a6502708d7037b2f683e15d3262315cdc1acc8fc91abedff320fcf16045`. No source, account, server state, build, or staging deployment was changed by this audit. Source and three curated 667×375 native stills were inspected. The focused Godot 4.7.2 headless story and cutscene scripts both passed without `SCRIPT ERROR` or `ERROR`. These checks assert story goals, twelve sequence records, some gates and frame layers; they do **not** play a fresh account through the story or measure animation/audio quality.

## Tool and acceptance status

The three original cinematic paintings were made with the built-in image generation tool and point-resized with ImageMagick; [asset provenance](../assets/cinematic-backgrounds-v1.md) records their source and hashes. Godot 4.7.2 `cinematic_living_plate.gdshader`, CanvasItem drawing, the existing boss atlas, dialogue portraits, music and SFX provide the actual motion/presentation. This follows the owner's preference for lightweight, editable, code/pixel-authored character actions rather than arbitrary generated character sheets. **Cinetic was installed as an offline authoring reference but has not produced a game clip or trailer**, and its full renderer stack has not been validated; [its README](../../tools/cinetic/README.md) also flags separate renderer/commercial terms. The screenplay's `ComfyColorGradePostFX` and `PixelWaterCausticsPostFX` are Phaser names; Godot uses its own shaders/environment instead.

The published staging smoke used a tutorial-complete fixture and proved ready-game navigation, not cutscene playback. No recorded, uninterrupted fresh-player browser run covers prologue → day-one Kedai → day-two garden → day-three Jalatunda → all six boss resonances → epilogue. Native stills establish composition, not action timing, audible cues, or Windows/Samsung S23 frame pacing. There is also a replay gap: a disconnect after the server records victory but before the local `victory` callback can cause reload to select only `epilogue`, omitting 4.3–4.4. Reloading a boss dive after resonance 3/6 can similarly miss the phase-two sequence. Cosmetic per-device seen flags alone cannot resolve those interrupted milestones.
