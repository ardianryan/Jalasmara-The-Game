# Visual clock correction and scene-entry continuity — 15 November 2026

The first land-lighting candidate smoothed its own 3D lights, but a server calendar update could still change other visuals in one frame. Opening Kedai Waras may advance the authoritative clock to 18:00; heartbeat corrections may move it backward. The pier lamps in `Main.gd`, forest lamps in `Forest.gd`, water/rock/actor lighting and `EnvironmentEffects` still read raw `daylight`. A newly created `CanvasModulate` also began at white before its first process tick.

This follow-up adds `presentedDaylight` and `presentedMinuteOfDay` to `EnvironmentState.snapshot()` for **visual consumers only**. They approach the raw clock with elapsed-time exponential smoothing, with a shortest cyclic route over midnight. The first authoritative calendar sync initializes the presentation directly to the server time so a new login/scene starts at its intended lighting. Subsequent shift and heartbeat corrections fade over several seconds. The existing `daylight`, `minuteOfDay`, `night`, calendar revision, pause, progress and API behavior remain raw and authoritative. Dialogue selection in `Main.gd` deliberately continues to use raw `daylight`.

Land and sea color, lanterns, water, rock and actor lighting now read the presented values, retaining a raw fallback for non-EnvironmentState fixtures. `EnvironmentEffects` initializes its tint from the current snapshot in `_ready`, including scene entry during an unfinished transition. The already-accepted ocean shaft shader receives presented daylight through `OceanBackdrop`; no new pass, asset, light, renderer, physics change or gameplay state is introduced. Per-frame work is two scalar interpolation updates and two snapshot fields. Real GPU frame time remains unmeasured.
