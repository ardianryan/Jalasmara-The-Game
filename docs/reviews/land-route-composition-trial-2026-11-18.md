# Land route composition trial — 18 November 2026

This isolated trial used `main` at `9b7ce47` as its base. It targeted the decorative dirt routes around the lower hub coast, central stairs, and forest stream bridge. No server floor, collision, route graph, map authoring data, or player movement code was changed. The source candidate was **rejected and reverted**; this commit contains the report and curated comparisons only. It was not built for Web, pushed, or staged.

The visual dirt routes are drawn from dozens of separately clipped, mitered polygons. The 667×375 baseline showed noisy joins and a few thin, disconnected strokes near the house and dock. A geometry diagnostic with Shapely 2.0.7 found that the 19 upper-court dirt pieces form one 91,830-square-unit route and three isolated remnants of 43, 18, and 555 square units. The candidate merged touching dirt polygons at the same elevation and priority, discarded decorative remnants under 900 square units, and drew a narrow muted-earth shoulder inside the merged footprint with the original dirt core. It reused the existing shader and authored map without adding a dependency or changing the server-owned walkable floor.

The focused Godot fixture passed: 42 hub dirt pieces became three retained joined footprints, and 20 forest pieces became one. It sampled the retained polygons for coverage, width, triangulation, and absence of newly expanded ground. Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility on Apple M2 then rendered the same six actual-world frames at 667×375 and 844×390 for the base and candidate. Both native passes printed `TERRAIN FACETS RENDER PASS` and emitted no `SCRIPT ERROR` or `ERROR:`. The fresh-worktree first editor import briefly logged missing font cache resources before import completed; the subsequent fixture and native captures were clean.

The candidate did remove the detached strokes, but the route became a broad, largely uniform orange band. At game size it was less integrated with the grass and adjacent buildings than the baseline, especially on the lower coast. This failed the visual acceptance criterion. The integrator independently reviewed the original-size 667×375 pair and agreed to reject it. Curated comparisons are [hub lower coast](../visual/land-route-parity-20261118/hub-lower-coast-ab-667x375.png) and [forest stream bridge](../visual/land-route-parity-20261118/forest-upper-west-bank-ab-667x375.png); left is base, right is candidate. Full 844×390 frames and other captures remain ignored under `artifacts/godot/land-route-{baseline,candidate,comparison}-20261118/` in the trial worktree.

Next pass should address the whole route composition rather than only the dither edge: refine authored widths and joins at the house and stairs, then add larger, low-frequency variation that belongs to the surrounding terrain. The isolated upper-court remnants remain a real source issue, but deleting them alone did not make the route moodboard-ready. Recheck both target sizes, actual walking near joins, and WebGL performance before accepting a future candidate. Native still frames do not establish Windows or Samsung S23 frame pacing. The only tools used here were the project Godot renderer and fixture, Python Pillow for comparison sheets, and Shapely for offline diagnosis; no external images or licensed assets were added.
