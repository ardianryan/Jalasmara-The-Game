# Reef 12 authored limestone underside — rejected

**Decision:** The connected underside trial is not suitable for the game. The six native views show a new flat, green-blue strip and dark seam below the playable shelf. Its painted holes do not make the rock masses read as one object; the large blue water wedge and the eastern vertical join remain. Runtime geometry and new art were reverted. This commit contains evidence and analysis only, with no change to collision, rendering, or distributed assets.

The isolated `work/reef12-authored-atlas-20261118` branch started at `5bd4e38`. The trial created a 512×128 RGBA source-pixel underside whose alpha silhouette, cavity rims, moss and fissures were drawn by `tools/reef12/build_underside.py`; a 28-color quantized pigment was derived from the project's own `ocean-terrain-atlas.png` crop `(0,174,512,302)`. A visual-only mesh in `TerrainGeometry.gd` followed the lower edge of terrain contour 12 from x4440 to x6144, receding from z−100 to z−235. The authoritative world contour and six resonance targets were not edited. This was a mesh-attached composition, not an imported moodboard card, but it still *looks* like a pasted-on strip at game size.

**Concrete source cause:** In `packages/shared/src/world.ts`, terrain 6 is a tall east-wall rectangle starting at world x5500, y1000; its generic `coastalContour` still has a mostly straight west boundary. At camera center x5500 and width 667, screen x≈340 maps to world x≈5506. This explains the abrupt vertical join in `deep_right`: it is the independent wall's mesh/atlas boundary, not just a repeat within the limestone image. Terrain 12 is a separate hand-authored shelf from world x4300 to x6144 around y2800–2958. Its lower contour is nearly level over most of that 1,844-unit run, and `TerrainGeometry._build_reef` extrudes its terraces using those same points; this produces the ruler-straight shelf. The wall and shelf share world-space UV sampling but **do not share edge vertices or depth continuity**. An underside strip attached only to terrain 12 therefore cannot repair either mesh junction. Earlier contour-only edits softened the wall boundary but left the shelf silhouette and water wedge.

The next candidate needs to replace the *visible faces of both terrain 6 and 12 together* with a single designed limestone junction or matched contiguous submeshes, authoring their top, front, underside and vertical terminal pixels/UVs as one composition. Keep the collision geometry as a separate authoritative layer; explicitly prove the visible silhouette does not imply a blocked swim lane. Build real undercut pockets into the shared face rather than extending another rectangle below it. The full six-camera sweep must show no straight lower line, hard x5500 atlas cut, pasted strip, or obstruction of Nala, 43 coral pivots/39 mounts, valve, and six targets before accepting source. A moving-camera check and browser/device pass would still be needed afterward.
