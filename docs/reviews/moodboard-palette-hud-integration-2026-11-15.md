# Land palette and compact HUD integration — 15 November 2026

The owner wants smooth time-of-day color and lighting, readable pixel art, and a HUD that leaves more gameplay visible. This package builds on the continuous-light staging release `d163bc2`. It changes the land grass and stone source colors, passes the existing smoothed dusk value into the single land grade shader, and shortens the upper-left identity badge on short landscape screens. Existing authored 32 px Bahari utility icons replace their smaller variants; touch targets remain at least 48 px. No geometry, collision, server calendar, gameplay rule, texture, shader pass, or third-party asset was added.

The [land before/after](../visual/land-palette-quality-hub-667x375-20261115.webp) compares identical 667×375 native hub frames. Grass is less lime, stone less pale, and the dusk path receives a warmer amber tone. The [HUD 667×375](../visual/moodboard-hud-badge-667x375-before-after-20261115.png) and [844×390](../visual/moodboard-hud-badge-844x390-before-after-20261115.png) component captures show the identity badge shrinking from 66.70 to 58.36 physical pixels and 69.31 to 60.65 pixels respectively, with text retained inside the brass edge. These are Godot native/component proofs, not public browser or physical-device acceptance.

Accepted source commits were cherry-picked from isolated worktrees as `41384c9` (land) and `ae8e0a9` (HUD).
