# Reef 12 authored wall and connected toe — rejected

**Decision:** Do not merge the terrain or runtime changes from this trial. At native game size the connected toe softens the hard vertical join, but a broad blue wedge remains beneath the reef shelf and the shelf's lower edge is still conspicuously straight. The result is a bounded visual improvement, not the requested cohesive 2.5D reef or moodboard parity. This commit contains diagnostic evidence only; no gameplay contour, collision, shader, or art asset is changed.

The isolated branch `work/reef12-authored-wall-20261118` started from `1a68c7237b5d6b49879c2c2e850b85e6f77c5317`. The rejected prototype replaced terrain contour 6's straight west termination with connected scalloped coves, including a toe reaching world x5445 near y2910. `TerrainGeometry.gd` temporarily expanded the render strip to cover that outline, clipped front polygons to the authoritative contour, and projected terrace vertices back to its boundary. Thus server blocking and the rendered wall were deliberately tested together, rather than disguising a collision gap with a floating sprite. The prototype reused the existing reef atlas; **it did not create purpose-authored limestone pixels**. It is not retained in source.

The baseline half comes from the earlier original-size Reef 12 diagnostic capture, while the trial half was rendered from this branch in Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility at the same camera center and viewport. These stills check the silhouette at two display sizes; they do not establish camera-motion quality or Samsung/Windows performance. Nala, coral mounts, and resonance fixtures remained visible in the trial captures. No moodboard image or third-party art was used in the comparison.

**Validation of the rejected prototype:** `npx tsx --test apps/api/test/reef-sanctuary-contour.test.ts` passed 2/2, including a temporary open-swim-lane test. `npm run typecheck` and `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/geographic-map-check.mjs` passed. A native geometry debug fixture reported `TERRAIN LAYERS PASS: 15 contours, 618 terrace bands, 5967 projected vertices` after front clipping. The final two Godot renders exited 0 without logged `SCRIPT ERROR` or `ERROR:`. A prior fixture iteration failed `Layered mesh escaped authoritative contour`; the clipping change resolved that diagnostic, but did not solve the art composition. The temporary test and geometry code are reverted. No full game, browser, or device acceptance is claimed.

**Next source-art approach:** Rebuild the shelf and wall as one purpose-authored limestone composition with a shared pixel grid and continuous UVs. Make a shaped underside and several connected rock masses around real open-water pockets; paint the terminal alpha silhouette and interior shadows together, then use the existing mesh only to carry those authored shapes. Inspect the full moving camera sweep at 667×375 and 844×390 before changing the authoritative contour. Keep the Nala swim corridor, coral mounts, resonance valve, and six boss targets unobstructed. A contour edit alone only moves the straight texture boundary; a second overlay card reads as pasted-on art.

Tools actually used: local Pillow for paired lossless WebP evidence, Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility for native capture, and project CPU tests. The paired images are original Jalasmara development evidence, not distributable game assets. Full PNG captures remain ignored under `artifacts/godot/reef12-toe-candidate/` in this isolated worktree for integrator review.
