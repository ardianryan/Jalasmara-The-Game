# Gito dock lookout: authored pixel pose, 18 November 2026

Status: **candidate on `work/resident-pixel-facing-20261118`**, based on local `main` `f01b223`. This is not yet merged, pushed, exported, or staged. It fixes one readable resident action; it does not establish full moodboard parity or directional art for all residents.

The previous raised-hand trial failed because the original partition leaves most of Gito's right arm in the torso mask. Moving the remaining short arm mask hid the elbow and made the hand look attached to his face. This candidate authors one full 96×128 source-grid pose, with continuous shoulder, sleeve, elbow, forearm and hand clusters. It reuses the project-owned Gito cutout and hand-places a small number of arm pixels using colors sampled from that cutout. The original source and walk/idle partition remain untouched. The pose appears only during Gito's scheduled `work_loop/inspect` phase, then the normal planted-foot rig resumes; the slingshot moves to his lowered left hand while he looks out. Character scale, route, collision, server state, and Nala are unchanged.
