## Ujang's west knee direction

The contact rig used screen-right as the knee bend hint for both east and west profiles. Thus the authored west head faced left while both knees bent behind that view. A new assertion measures the actual upper/lower Sprite3D quad endpoints, projects the knee away from its hip–ankle axis, and checks the signed component against the displayed profile. It reproduces **836 backward west knees** on the old runtime, with zero unreachable-foot samples. The failed log is preserved.

The one-line runtime change selects screen-left for west and retains the other hints. It does not reflect the rig, exchange the wooden right leg, change actor motion, move a planted sole, scale limbs or relax contact limits. The registered default Ujang fixture now includes the signed-mesh assertion. Its native turn shortcut also rejects failed knees, writes every sequential pose to JSON and uses `force_draw` to avoid waiting indefinitely for an occluded desktop window.

After the runtime fix, headless contact checks pass all 1,242 steps, 168 sharp turns and 26 independent rod grips.
