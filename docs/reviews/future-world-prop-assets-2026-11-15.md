# Future-world prop asset review — 15 November 2026

**Candidate art only.** The owner requested prepared art for solar and generator power at the director's RV and separate inspector quarters, Saras's research/IT field camp, and a future northern plantation/fire state. We added 13 code-drawn PNG sprites, an asset manifest, source generator, alpha/hash test and [transparent-asset contact sheet](../visual/future-world-props-v1-20261115.png). No object was placed in Phase 1 or attached to a cutscene; the forest remains intact and the north route blocked.

The canvas/pivot contract matches [Plan 11](../plans/11-future-land-world-setup.md). `forest_fire_sheet` is an additional four-frame asset alongside the plan's smoke sheet. Frame zero supports reduced motion. The source and parameters are editable in [`generate.py`](../../tools/future-world-art/generate.py); outputs sum to about 12 KiB. The separate cabin has no assigned map coordinate. Solar and generator visuals do not assert powered gameplay systems. Saras's satellite terminal is generic and carries no Starlink logo.

Validation: `python3 tools/future-world-art/generate.py` completed; `python3 tools/future-world-art/test_assets.py` passed 2 tests. The contact sheet was inspected at 1× and 2× pixel size for 3/4 volume, clear transparent outlines, distinct palm fruit and legible equipment. Godot import, actual 667×375 gameplay readability, device performance and connected-floor placement remain untested and must be checked by the integration package. The sheet is an authoring preview, not a screenshot from staging.

Provenance: shapes and pixels were drawn with project code using Pillow 11.3.0 offline; no AI image output, external sprite atlas, moodboard pixels or licensed third-party game art. PNGs use Jalasmara's [game-art terms](../../LICENSE), while the generator uses the source-code MIT terms. All output SHA-256 values are recorded in the runtime manifest. No change to `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md` is needed for these project-owned pixels.
