# Underwater pipe props: grounded visual placement

Date: 15 November 2026. Isolated branch `work/undersea-pipe-grounding-20261115` based on `ba38462`. This is a **local source candidate**, not a staging release.

The native baseline showed the disposal valve hovering about one displayed prop-height above the lower reef shelf; the broken elbow also read as detached from its upper ledge. `OceanActors.gd` now positions the valve's existing atlas cell at world offset `(0,+82)` instead of `(0,+18)`, placing its base 20.5 world units above the underlying reef top so the sprite's rubble overlaps the shelf in the actual rendered view. The broken elbow moves from `(-13,+12)` to `(+35,+25)`, seating its base and right flange into the neighboring rock ledge. The corporate-pipe evidence marker remains on its existing ledge. No sprite was stretched, no new texture, shader, model, light, collider, hitbox, map geometry or server coordinate was added. The valve remains 82 units from its authoritative interaction anchor, within its existing 140-unit radius; the leak visual remains within its hazard radius.
