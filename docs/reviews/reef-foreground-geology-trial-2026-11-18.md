# Reef 12 foreground geology trial — 18 November 2026

**Decision: rejected; documentation only.** The trial branch started from `e839ff4`. No renderer, test, shared terrain, server collision, or boss gameplay change from this package should be integrated.

The defect is the straight dark boundary across `ReefRelief12` beneath the waste valve. I kept the authoritative 13-point contour unchanged and added seven joined, atlas-shaded rock faces to its existing mesh. All 56 new triangles were inside the server-blocked polygon, 73–78 world units toward the camera, with no additional mesh or material. The temporary focused Godot fixture verified bounded triangles, all projected vertices inside the original contour, unchanged world JSON, 43 coral pivots and 39 stone mounts. Godot 4.7.2 headless passed.

Native Apple M2 captures used the same full-ocean fixture, `balanced` quality, player/camera state, oxygen 77, and coins 700. The [667 × 375 baseline](../visual/reef-contour-parity-20261118/baseline-667x375.png) and [844 × 390 baseline](../visual/reef-contour-parity-20261118/baseline-844x390.png) are source-equivalent because the intervening `e839ff4` commit only added documentation. The [667 × 375 trial](../visual/reef-foreground-geology-20261118/rejected-face-667x375.png) and [844 × 390 trial](../visual/reef-foreground-geology-20261118/rejected-face-844x390.png) show small internal value changes, but the horizontal boundary remains continuous and visually dominant. The coral roots remain grounded; the platform still looks artificially flat. This is not a meaningful improvement against the moodboard, so the runtime code and focused fixture were reverted before commit.

The result confirms a geometric limit: drawing entirely inside the blocked polygon can vary its face but cannot change its top screen-space boundary. Decorative faces may help a later composition redesign, but this specific patch does not solve the silhouette. A viable next attempt needs a joined geology/foreground composition that masks the line without opening a fake swim corridor, while preserving server clearance and adjusting coral support where required. Do not revive the previously rejected long teal cove or alter the six boss targets to hide the defect.

The first temporary direct-deep render harness had a local parse error caused by constants placed before `extends`; this was corrected. The direct-deep 667 process then emitted no first frame after 60 seconds and was stopped. The unchanged full-ocean fixture immediately completed both shallow and deep captures at 667 × 375 and 844 × 390 with no `ERROR:` or `SCRIPT ERROR`; the initial fresh-worktree editor import also completed. The transient harness failures are not evidence of a production script error. Native stills do not prove browser motion or physical Windows/Samsung S23 performance.
