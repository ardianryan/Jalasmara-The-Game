# Short-landscape HUD visibility — 13 November 2026

After the owner accepted Nala's visible walking, the current source passed the [one-player full-day regression](full-day-gait-regression-2026-11-13.md). Its 844×390 screenshot showed the action label spanning the middle-right play area and a prominent centered minimap. This item-4 pass changes only the Godot HUD layout in short landscape windows (physical height ≤480 px and width greater than height):

- Ordinary interaction labels use **280 logical units** instead of 380; keyboard scaling makes that 280 physical px at 844×390, while the 667×375 touch fixture uses about 195 physical px. If a real long label would need more than two lines, it expands only then to the previous 380-unit bound. The long “Bersihkan & Amati: Aliran Hulu Bandar” fixture remains fully readable above the action button. The action and net touch targets are unchanged.
- The minimap diameter is **68 px** instead of 80 px, while the 960×540 desktop minimap remains 128 px.
- Four-second notices are capped at **320 px** instead of 360 px and dock below the right-side utility/weather cluster. This leaves the center of the play area clear and keeps notices away from the left-side oxygen instrument. Their full text is still shown; the 667×375 long-message fixture is about 87 px tall and remains under the 100 px bound.

The existing [`hud-short-landscape.gd`](../../scripts/tests/hud-short-landscape.gd) now asserts the physical minimap/notice/ordinary-prompt bounds, long-prompt readability, separation from the identity panel and minimap, and a ≥48 px touch action. A native Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility capture at [667×375 touch landscape](../visual/hud-visibility-667x375-20261113.png) is original HUD component output, PNG SHA-256 `59331b3e756f998cca4f93083c020ae128e4e27bec7eeea915f6fca1de802d6d`. It shows the smaller map and right-docked three-line notice. The blank background means it is a layout proof, not a gameplay screenshot or physical-device usability test.
