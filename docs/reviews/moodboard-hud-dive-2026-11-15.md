# Compact dive gauge at phone landscape size

The owner asked for the game to approach the supplied cozy pixel-art moodboard without making the HUD obstruct gameplay. At 667×375 touch landscape, the existing oxygen/depth/bag frame occupied a large top-left block. This candidate gives that viewport a compact layout: oxygen remains the focal number, the verified oxygen bar stays visible, and depth plus bag weight share one legible row. Portrait, desktop and non-dive layouts retain their existing arrangement. No gameplay, oxygen rule, touch target or art asset changed.

The first 94-pixel draft failed visual review: the oxygen value touched the bar and depth/weight landed on the frame's bottom ornament. A later 112-pixel draft passed the short-landscape fixture but failed the broader oxygen regression because the number's Godot control bounds still touched the bar. The final 116-pixel layout puts label, number and maximum on one row, reserves the same caption slot for temporary O₂-loss feedback, and gives the bar and depth/weight their own space. The [native 667×375 capture](../visual/moodboard-hud-dive-native-667x375-20261115.png) has SHA-256 `017e71646cc03ed9cc7cdda25311aa757aedf0ccba7a243e216e06c962e1d998`. This is a HUD component fixture on a solid background, not an in-world browser screenshot or physical S23 acceptance.

Godot 4.7.2 headless and native `scripts/tests/hud-short-landscape.gd` passed at 844×390 keyboard, 667×375 touch and 960×540 keyboard. `scripts/tests/hud-oxygen.gd` passed its portrait, landscape, critical oxygen, loss-feedback and bar/label non-overlap cases after the final correction. The extended short-landscape test bounds the phone gauge to 290×122 physical pixels, checks that depth and weight do not overlap and remain on one line, and enforces at least 16 physical pixels for the depth font. The native final log had no `SCRIPT ERROR` or `ERROR`; `git diff --check` passed. The first isolated-worktree attempt lacked Godot imports and `.runtime/world-map.json`; after importing and copying only that generated test fixture, the test passed. This initial environment failure is not a runtime game defect.

Tooling: Godot 4.7.2 Compatibility native/headless, no new dependency or license. Source is `GameHud.gd`; the existing Bahari HUD artwork is reused. Next gate after integration: inspect this frame in an actual dive at 667×375 and 844×390, including low oxygen and boss action states, then check Windows/S23 visibility and frame pacing. This package alone does not establish full visual parity with the moodboard.
