# Roadmap

This is a working order of acceptance, not a promise of release dates. A feature is complete only when the evidence in [PRD](docs/PRD.md) and the relevant review report passes on an identified build. The [active handoff](docs/agent-handoff.md) controls current ownership and blockers.

| Stage | Outcome | Acceptance focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.1.0 baseline | Playable staging prototype | Authoritative progress, village/forest/dive loop, public startup and route smoke, rollback-ready deployment |
| Core movement | Natural Nala gait and grounded traversal | Tracked fixtures; flat ground and stairs/ramp in all required directions; actual moving native/browser evidence; modular gear stays aligned |
| World finishing | Cohesive village, forest, and sea | Three-quarter building alignment, natural terrain and shore transitions, supported rocks/swim gaps, readable canopy, moving current/foam, moodboard-based color review |
| Interaction and comfort | HUD, dialogue, loading, and controls | Compact night HUD, readable quizzes, analog joystick, fullscreen/safe areas, actual byte progress, keyboard/touch and reduced-motion checks |
| Quality and reliability | Playtest and performance | Complete human playthrough, Windows/S23 physical-device measurements, save migration, regression and security review |

The separate [WorldKit](https://git.ppti.me/ardianryan/jalasmara-worldkit) remains an offline authoring aid. Its drafts and reference screenshots are not automatically accepted into the running world.
