# Support

For reproducible bugs, use a Gitea issue with the build/source SHA, browser or device, steps, expected result, actual result, and a screenshot if safe. Check the [development status](docs/development-status.md) and [open work](TODO.md) first; this prototype has known unfinished areas.

For setup or contribution questions, use a discussion or issue in the [Gitea repository](https://git.ppti.me/ardianryan/Jalasmara-The-Game). For security concerns, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not post exploit details publicly. Do not include passwords, tokens, private moodboards, or player data in support reports.

Staging is a development environment, not a guaranteed service. Save compatibility and availability may change during development.
