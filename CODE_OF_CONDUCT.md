# Code of conduct

We want Jalasmara's development spaces to be useful and respectful to contributors, players, educators, and the communities whose cultural traditions inspire the game.

Treat people with respect. Discuss work and evidence without personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, threats, or unwanted sexual attention. Credit authors and upstream projects accurately. Do not publish private messages, account details, or personal information without consent. Be open to corrections about cultural references and avoid presenting fictional adaptations as historical fact.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a contact channel listed on the owner's profile. If no private channel is available, ask publicly for one without naming affected people or sharing sensitive details. Reports will be reviewed with discretion; confidentiality cannot be promised beyond the people needed to respond.

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, and project-related community spaces.
